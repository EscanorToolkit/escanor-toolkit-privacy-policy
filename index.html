Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Escanor Toolkit provides hero information, stats, builds, guides, and related content. The current version does not require an account or include advertising or analytics SDKs. It does not request access to your contacts, precise location, camera, or microphone.

Online content

The app connects to GitHub and image-hosting services to load content. These providers receive your IP address and standard request information when delivering files. Their processing and retention practices follow their own privacy policies, including the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Local storage & deletion

Content and images are cached on your device to improve loading. Cached data stays until refreshed or removed by the app, Android, or you. You can clear it through Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.

Security

App content is downloaded over HTTPS. We do not sell personal information. No electronic transmission or storage method is completely secure.

Planned features

Future updates are planned to introduce Google AdMob advertising, optional Shizuku-assisted functionality, and wallpaper downloads. These features are not available in the current version.

This policy will be updated to explain their data handling, third-party services, and permissions before they become available. Shizuku access will require explicit user authorization.
